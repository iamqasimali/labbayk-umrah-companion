Before tomorrow

Ihram rules and checklist

While in Ihram, avoid

  • Perfume or anything scented: soap, wipes, cream, scented food
  • Cutting or pulling out hair or nails
  • Marriage or proposing, intimate talk or relations
  • Arguing, quarrelling and every sin
  • Hunting or harming animals
  • Men: stitched clothes, covering the head or face, footwear covering the middle bone of the foot
  • Women: niqab and gloves. If needed, hold a cloth that does not touch the face
  • Rubbing the body so hair falls, or wiping the face with a towel

Allowed in Ihram

  • Bathing gently, without rubbing off dirt
  • Ring, watch, glasses, money belt, hearing aid
  • Umbrella, miswak, unscented kohl
  • Filing a broken nail, a bandage, pulling a tooth if needed
  • Changing into fresh Ihram sheets
  • A blanket while sleeping (men keep head and face uncovered) and a pillow
  • Killing a dangerous animal

The five Miqats

  • Dhul Hulaifah (Abyar Ali): coming from Madinah
  • Al-Juhfah (Rabigh): from Syria and Egypt
  • Qarn al-Manazil: from Najd
  • Yalamlam: from Yemen
  • Dhat Irq: from Iraq and Iran

Flying from Pakistan

If you fly straight to Jeddah, you cross the Miqat in the air. Wear Ihram at home or at the airport, pray 2 rak'ahs, and make the niyyah when the crew announces the Miqat (about 15 to 30 minutes before landing). If you go to Madinah first, wear Ihram later at Dhul Hulaifah (Masjid Shajarah) on the way to Makkah.
