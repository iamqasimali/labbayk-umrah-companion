Before tomorrow

Ihram rules and checklist

While in Ihram, avoid

  • Perfume or scented soap, on body or clothes
  • Cutting hair or nails
  • Men: stitched clothing, covering the head
  • Women: niqab and gloves (cover the face with a loose cloth if needed)
  • Arguing, bad words, and marital relations
  • Hunting or harming animals

Flying from Pakistan

If you fly straight to Jeddah, you cross the Miqat in the air. Wear Ihram at home or at the airport, pray 2 rak'ahs, and make the niyyah when the crew announces the Miqat (about 30 minutes before landing). If you go to Madinah first, wear Ihram later at Dhul Hulaifah (Masjid Shajarah) on the way to Makkah.
